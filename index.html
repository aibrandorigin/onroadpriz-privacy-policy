Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information when you use the OnRoadPriz app and services.

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OnRoadPriz ("OnRoadPriz", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the OnRoadPriz mobile application and related website and services (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy.

Important: The Services may be used to browse, create, and manage vehicle listings. Please do not submit information that is unnecessary for using the Services, and do not include sensitive personal information in public listing descriptions or photographs.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact and account information: your name, mobile number, email address, and information you provide when registering, signing in, or contacting us.
  • Vehicle listing information: details about a vehicle that you choose to submit, such as its make, model, year, condition, price, description, and related photographs or other listing content.
  • Location information: location information you provide or permit the app or device to share, where location features are available. You can manage app location permissions through your device settings. If you deny permission, some location-based features may not work as intended.
  • Communications: information contained in messages, support requests, feedback, or other communications you send to us.
  • Technical information: limited information that may be made available by your device, browser, or hosting environment, such as IP address, device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic or log information, where collected by the Services or their infrastructure.

We do not intend to collect precise location continuously or in the background unless a feature clearly explains this and you grant the relevant permission.

2. How we use information

We may use information for purposes such as:

  • creating and managing user accounts and providing the Services;
  • publishing, displaying, maintaining, and managing vehicle listings and photographs;
  • providing location-related functionality where enabled;
  • responding to enquiries, support requests, and user communications;
  • maintaining the safety, security, and reliability of the Services;
  • detecting misuse, fraud, spam, or activity that may violate our terms;
  • meeting legal obligations and enforcing our rights; and
  • improving the Services using information available to us.

3. Vehicle listings and public information

Information you choose to include in a vehicle listing, including photographs and vehicle details, may be visible to other users or visitors according to the listing and account settings available in the Services. Do not upload photographs that reveal personal documents, home addresses, number plates, faces, or other information unless you intend to share it. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to upload and share the content you submit.

4. How information may be shared

We may disclose information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users: listing information that you choose to publish may be displayed to people using or visiting the Services.
  • Service providers: with providers that help us host, operate, secure, maintain, or support the Services, but only as reasonably necessary for their services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where disclosure is required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the Services, or our rights.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, restructuring, financing, or transfer of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • With your direction: when you ask us to share information or otherwise consent to a disclosure.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing unless we explain this to you and obtain any consent required by law.

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain account and listing records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal obligations, and protect the Services. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or de-identify it, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping needs.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and verification of your request, you may contact us to:

  • request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information;
  • request deletion of your account and associated information;
  • withdraw permission for location access through your device settings; and
  • raise a concern about how your information is handled.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, dispute-resolution, or record-keeping purposes. Deleting the app from your device does not necessarily delete your account or information stored on our systems; please contact us to request account or data deletion.

8. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Third-party services and links

The Services may rely on third-party infrastructure or contain links to third-party websites or services. Those third parties may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies. This Policy does not cover the independent practices of third parties.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or seek consent for material changes.
