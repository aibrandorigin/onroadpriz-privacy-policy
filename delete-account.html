OnRoadPriz support

Request account deletion

If you no longer want to use OnRoadPriz, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting our support team by email.

How to submit your request

  1. Send an email to aibrandorigin@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line OnRoadPriz Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the email address registered with your OnRoadPriz account, if you have access to it.
  4. Include your registered name and mobile number so our team can identify and verify the account.
Email an account deletion request

What happens next?

Our support team will review your request and may contact you to verify that you own the account. Once verified, we will process the account deletion in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our data-retention obligations.

Data deletion and retention

We will delete or de-identify personal data associated with the account where applicable. Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required or permitted by law, or where necessary for legitimate purposes such as resolving disputes, preventing fraud, or maintaining security.

For your security, do not include your password, one-time password (OTP), or other login credentials in your email.

Need help?

Contact aibrandorigin@gmail.com for assistance with an account deletion request.
